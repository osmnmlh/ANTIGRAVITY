# NASA sources relevant to ANTIGRAVITY

NASA material that relates to astronaut health monitoring and to the hazards our app watches. Each URL was found
through a search of nasa.gov / spaceappschallenge.org on 2026-11-15. Entries marked **(used)** are already cited in the repo;
the rest are recommended further reading.

> **What we did and did not take from NASA.** We took the **five-hazard framing** of NASA's Human Research Program and
> map every indicator to a hazard. Our green/amber/red thresholds are **generic adult reference ranges chosen for the demo**,
> not NASA limits; the 200 mSv mission radiation budget is a **demo value**. NASA numbers such as the 600 mSv career limit
> appear only as background context in the radiation copy. This is demo software, not a medical device.
